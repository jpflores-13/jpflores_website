Scintilla is a browser-based tool I built for practitioners and design thinking students who want a structured way to develop and test innovative ideas.

It's organized around the five-step process from *The Experimentation Field Book*:

1. **Frame** — define testable ideas clearly
2. **Evidence** — specify what would count as proof
3. **Test** — choose the right method for the question
4. **Prototype** — build something learnable
5. **Iterate** — analyze results and loop back

The app has three main workspaces. The **Dashboard** gives a portfolio view of all your concepts, showing where each one sits in the pipeline and what evidence you've gathered. You can toggle between a mission control list view and a kanban-style pipeline view.

The **Guided Workflow** walks you through each of the five steps with editable templates, a value/effort matrix for positioning concepts by impact and effort, and a Test Decision Flow that gives live recommendations on what kind of test to run based on your current question.

The **Systems Mapping** workspace is what I'm most proud of. It includes four dedicated tools — a Systems Support Map, a Feedback Loops explorer, a 5Rs Diagnostic, and an Archetypes gallery — for understanding the broader systemic context around an idea before committing to a test.

Everything runs in the browser and persists automatically in local storage, so there's no account required and no data leaves your machine.
